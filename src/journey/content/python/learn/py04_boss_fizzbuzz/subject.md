# Boss: Fizz Buzz

Level 4 · boss

## Assignment

Go through the numbers 1 to 15, printing one line for each:

- a multiple of 3 prints `Fizz`
- a multiple of 5 prints `Buzz`
- a multiple of both prints `FizzBuzz`
- anything else prints the number itself

The output starts like this:

    1
    2
    Fizz
    4
    Buzz

## What you need to know

Everything from levels 1 to 4. Test the "both" case first, or `15` will print
`Fizz` and never reach the others. `elif` chains are checked from the top and
stop at the first one that matches.
